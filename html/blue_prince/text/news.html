[1985.08.22] Marigold Wins Herald Literary Honor

Popular childrens writer Marion Marigold adds yet another feather to her scholastic cap when she discovered on Thursday that her picture book "Swin Bird" had been chosen as the Herald Literary Medal illustrated book of the year.
